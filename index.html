NOVEMBER 2025 – PRESENT

FROM KERALA TO CANTERBURY

Today I am a General Case Manager at the Ministry of Social Development — a role I began in November 2025.

I manage a varied caseload, work with agencies and stakeholders, and keep records accurate and compliant. I make decisions carefully against policy, with respect for Te Tiriti o Waitangi and culturally responsive practice.

Day to day, I coordinate case files through to resolution, run compliance checks, and prepare defensible files for Reviews of Decision. I schedule meetings across parties, review documents carefully, and work under pressure while adapting to changing priorities.

I embed Te Ao Māori principles and mātauranga Māori in my casework, aligned with Te Tiriti o Waitangi obligations. Before MSD, I worked in community support, aged care coordination, and hotel front-of-house — experience that still shapes how I work with people under pressure.

WHERE I’M GOING.

EMPLOYMENT CASE MANAGEMENT

Employment Case Management is where I am heading — not a title I hold today. Today I am a Case Manager (General Case Manager) at the Ministry of Social Development. Ahead of me is a deliberate progression toward work that centres employment pathways and lasting outcomes for the people I support.

Employment pathways

I want to help people identify realistic, sustainable routes toward work — grounded in their circumstances, capability and aspirations.

Vocational support

I want to understand barriers clearly, then build capability through practical, person-centred support.

Employer engagement

I want to connect skills, people and opportunities — bridging the gap between readiness and work.

Sustainable employment

I want to focus on outcomes that last: not just placement, but progress that holds.
