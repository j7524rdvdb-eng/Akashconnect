About

My story, in plain words

I am first and foremost someone who works with people. My path started with a Graduate Diploma in Supply Chain & Logistics at Ara Institute of Canterbury. From there I took on kitchen and front-office work at Distinction Hotel in Christchurch, then moved into caregiving — first in Nelson, then back in Christchurch — and into support work with IDEA Services and Te Whare Ngakau Trust.

Each role taught me how to listen carefully, keep accurate records, stay calm under pressure, and help people take the next practical step. That is the thread that runs through everything I do.
