00 / THE CITY

CHRISTCHURCH IS THE CITY.

I did not arrive into a generic New Zealand. I arrived into Christchurch — Ōtautahi — rebuilt streets, the Avon / Ōtākaro cutting through the centre, Port Hills on the skyline, and a city still teaching me how to belong.

The landmarks below are not places I claim as workplaces. They are the geography of home: the frame around every chapter that follows — from Distinction Hotel in the central city, to aged care and community support across Christchurch, to case management that still sits in this place.

CENTRE

Cathedral Square

The city’s pulse — and the Cardboard Cathedral nearby, a reminder that Christchurch rebuilds with intention.

RIVER

The river that threads the city. When I need quiet, this is the line I follow through town.

HORIZON

Port Hills

The ridge that holds the city. On clear evenings the view still feels like arrival.

01 / CURRENT · CHRISTCHURCH

CASE MANAGER

GENERAL CASE MANAGER · MINISTRY OF SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT · NEW ZEALAND

NOVEMBER 2025 – PRESENT

CHRISTCHURCH · THE CITY

Today I am a General Case Manager at the Ministry of Social Development — a role I began in November 2025, grounded in Christchurch.

I manage a varied caseload, work with agencies and stakeholders, and keep records accurate and compliant. I make decisions carefully against policy, with respect for Te Tiriti o Waitangi and culturally responsive practice.

Day to day, I coordinate case files through to resolution, run compliance checks, and prepare defensible files for Reviews of Decision. I schedule meetings across parties, review documents carefully, and work under pressure while adapting to changing priorities.

I embed Te Ao Māori principles and mātauranga Māori in my casework, aligned with Te Tiriti o Waitangi obligations. Before MSD, I worked in community support, aged care coordination, and hotel front-of-house across this city — experience that still shapes how I work with people under pressure.

Employment Case Management is where I am heading — not a title I hold today. Today I am a Case Manager (General Case Manager) at the Ministry of Social Development.
