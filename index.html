The journey

Where it began

My journey to Aotearoa began with a Level 7 Diploma in Supply Chain and Logistics at Ara Institute of Canterbury — a qualification that gave me a deep appreciation for the systems that keep the world moving: planning, coordination, and accountability. Little did I know that these same principles would shape how I approach every role I’ve held since.

Chapters

Experience

  1. 01

    Education

    Ara Institute of Canterbury

    Level 7 Diploma in Supply Chain and Logistics — grounding in planning, coordination, and accountability that still shapes every role.

  2. 02

    Hospitality

    Distinction Hotel Christchurch

    Started as a Kitchen Assistant — no job too small, every detail worth getting right. That attitude was noticed, and management moved me to the Front Office, where I discovered a genuine passion for connecting with people, resolving challenges, and making others feel welcome.

  3. 03

    Frontline

    COVID-19 response

    When the hotel became a government-managed isolation facility, I stayed on the frontlines. Supporting people isolated far from their families, I understood that care is not just a profession — it is a calling. The New Zealand Government’s COVID-19 Response Recognition Award was a humbling affirmation of that purpose.

  4. 04

    Aged care

    Summerset & Chatswood

    Caregiver at Summerset Retirement Village, then Caregiver Coordinator at Chatswood Retirement Village. Overseeing care delivery, managing schedules, and supporting staff deepened my leadership and reinforced a commitment to dignity and compassion in service.

  5. 05

    Support work

    Tāngata whai ora

    As a Support Worker, I supported tāngata whai ora — people living with intellectual disabilities and mental health needs. I assisted with daily living, managed medications, and drew on Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, and English to build trust across cultures. Every interaction reminded me that to be truly helpful, you must first make someone feel seen.

  6. 06

    Today

    MSD Kaikōura & HSR

    As a Case Manager at the Ministry of Social Development in Kaikōura, these threads have come together — the logistics mindset, the hospitality instinct, and the care-sector heart. Alongside this, I serve as Health and Safety Representative (HSR) for the Kaikōura Work and Income office.

Where I stand

Today in Kaikōura

Case Manager

At the Ministry of Social Development in Kaikōura, I bring together planning, coordination, and a care-first approach — helping people navigate support with dignity and clarity.

Health & Safety Representative

As HSR for the Kaikōura Work and Income office, I represent workers on health and safety matters — identifying and raising workplace risks, supporting consultation, and promoting a safe and healthy working environment. This role has strengthened my leadership, advocacy, communication, and commitment to creating a positive workplace for others.
