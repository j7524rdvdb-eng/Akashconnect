My journey

Five steps

I started with study and hotel work, then moved into aged care and support work. Now I work in case management at MSD.

Timeline

  1. 01

    01 · Study

    Ara Institute of Canterbury

    I completed a Level 7 Diploma in Supply Chain and Logistics. That taught me how to plan, coordinate, and stay organised — skills I still use every day.

  2. 02

    02 · Hotels

    Distinction Hotel Christchurch

    I worked in the kitchen, then moved to front office. When the hotel became a COVID isolation facility, I stayed on. I received the New Zealand Government’s COVID-19 Response Recognition Award.

  3. 03

    03 · Aged care

    Summerset & Chatswood

    I was a caregiver at Summerset Retirement Village, then a Caregiver Coordinator at Chatswood. I looked after residents, managed rosters, and focused on treating people with dignity.

  4. 04

    04 · Support work

    Tāngata whai ora

    I supported people with intellectual disabilities and mental health needs — helping with daily living and medication. I worked in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, and English.

  5. 05

    05 · Today

    MSD & HSR

    I am a Case Manager at the Ministry of Social Development, and the Health and Safety Representative for my Work and Income office. The planning, hospitality, and care skills from earlier roles still guide how I work.
